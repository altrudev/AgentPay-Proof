WHY IT EXISTS

A payment receipt isn't enough.

Blockchains can prove money moved. They don't prove an autonomous agent was allowed to make that purchase—or that the service delivered what the agent paid for.

01

Bounded authority

Amount, service, recipient, asset and network are constrained before payment.

02

Independent observation

The purchasing agent's own account is never treated as sufficient evidence.

03

Portable proof

Intent through outcome is bound into evidence that can be independently checked.
